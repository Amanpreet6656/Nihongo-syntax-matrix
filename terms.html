Effective Date: October 17, 2026

1. Educational Purpose & Academic Scope

The Nihongo Syntax Matrix is an open educational tool created for university students, educators, and independent scholars of the Japanese language. Its primary function is to model Japanese morphological inflection and syntactic rules in an austere, distraction-free environment.

2. Permitted Use & Classroom Deployment

Instructors and learners are permitted to use this application for non-commercial instruction, linguistic research, study sessions, and offline coursework. You may distribute and mirror the source files within educational environments under standard open-access principles.

3. Algorithmic Integrity & Lexical Scope

The algorithmic rules implemented in this application model standard Tokyo Japanese (共通語 / 標準語) phonological shifts, including standard euphony (促音便, イ音便, 撥音便) and classical Five-Grade (Godan) vowel alternations. While developed with rigorous attention to linguistic precision, regional dialectal variations and rare archaic colloquialisms are outside the primary scope of this tool.

This software is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind, express or implied. The developers assume no liability for academic examinations, translations, or scholarly publications prepared without secondary reference to primary reference grammars.

5. Modifications & Source Distribution

Users may inspect, modify, and adapt the underlying vanilla ECMAScript algorithms for personal pedagogical research or classroom laboratory demonstration.
