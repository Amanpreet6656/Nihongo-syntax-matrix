Syntactic Constraints & Particle Mechanics

Temporal Marker に (ni)

Absolute vs Relative Time Rule

The case particle に (ni) marks an absolute, mathematically defined temporal point on the calendar. Conversely, relative temporal nouns (今日, 明日, 来週, 去年) strictly reject に.

✓ 2026年10月17日に会議があります。 Grammatically required: Fixed calendar coordinate
✕ 明日に会議があります。 (Unnatural) Syntax violation: Relative deictic noun blocks particle に
Span Markers から / まで

Temporal Boundaries (Source & Goal)

The postpositional particles から (kara) and まで (made) define the initial boundary (ablatival) and terminating boundary (terminative) of an interval.

✓ 10月10日から10月17日まで研修を行います。 Bounded interval: From the 10th until the 17th
Calendar Date vs. Duration Counter

Point vs Interval Counter Morphology

A calendar date identifies a precise location on the calendar, whereas duration expressions append the counter interval suffix 間 (kan).

・ 1日 (ついたち / tsuitachi) = 1st day of the month Point on calendar: Archaic 'tsukitachi' (moon rise)
・ 1日間 (いちにちかん / ichinichikan) = For one day Temporal duration: Sino-Japanese reading + 間 suffix

Gojūon Phonology Matrix & Verb Dan Association

The classical Japanese phonetic grid (五十音図) mapping the five vowel grades (段: a, i, u, e, o) to Godan verbal stem transformations.

Structural Connection to Godan Inflection

In Japanese grammatical pedagogy, Godan (五段) literally translates to "five grades" or "five steps". A Group 1 verb shifts its base consonant across all five vowel rows of the syllabary:

  • あ段 (a-grade): Irrealis stem (未然形) used for negative -nai, passive -reru, and causative -seru. (Note: Endings in -u shift to -wa).
  • い段 (i-grade): Continuative stem (連用形) used for polite -masu, compound verbs, and noun conversion.
  • う段 (u-grade): Terminal / Attributive form (終止形 / 連体形), the standard citation or dictionary form.
  • え段 (e-grade): Imperative (命令形) and Hypothetical stem (仮定形) for conditional -ba and potential verbs.
  • お段 (o-grade): Volitional stem (意向形) combined with -u (e.g., 書く → 書こう / kakō).
